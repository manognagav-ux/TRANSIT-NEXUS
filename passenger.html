COMMUNITY-POWERED UPDATES

Missed a check-in?
Help us update it.

If a conductor has missed updating the bus at a stop, you can submit a passenger report. Reports are labelled as passenger-submitted and are not treated as staff-verified updates.

Submitting a report does not verify a bus location. Reports may need staff confirmation.
OUR APPROACH

People-powered.
Clearly labelled.

Staff check-ins and passenger reports are different information sources. TRANSIT NEXUS keeps that distinction visible so passengers can judge how recent and reliable an update may be.
